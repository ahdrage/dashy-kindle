# Device setup

The tested device is a **Kindle Paperwhite 1 Wi-Fi (B024)** on **5.6.1.1 (268989035)**. Native Dashy and the final landscape orientation, with the USB/power edge on the left, were confirmed working on 9 September 2026.

The Kindle OS remains installed. Kinduino temporarily stops the reader interface while Dashy runs. Automatic startup is prepared but disabled and unverified.
